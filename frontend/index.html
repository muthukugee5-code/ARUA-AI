FAQ

Frequently asked questions

How does ARUA AI generate images?
ARUA AI uses cutting-edge AI models including Flux through Pollinations.ai to generate high-quality images from text descriptions. Our smart prompt enhancement system automatically improves your prompts for better results.
Is ARUA AI free to use?
Yes! You can start for free with 100 AI credits per month, which lets you generate 100 images. Upgrade to Pro or Enterprise for more credits, higher quality, and advanced features.
Do I own the images I generate?
Yes, you own all images you generate with ARUA AI. You can use them for personal or commercial projects without attribution required on paid plans.
What AI models does ARUA AI use?
We use Flux (the most advanced open-source image model) through Pollinations.ai, and Hugging Face models for prompt enhancement. We continuously update to include the latest state-of-the-art models.
Can I use ARUA AI for commercial projects?
Absolutely. Pro and Enterprise subscribers get full commercial usage rights for all generated content. Free tier users can use images for personal projects.
